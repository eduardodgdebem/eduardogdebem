# CLAUDE.md — Eduardo de Bem's site (branch `exp/classical-cyber`)

A from-scratch redesign, after Nous Research's Hermes Agent and Portal sites: classical statues, engravings and paintings printed in one green ink on a green-black night, beside light condensed headlines, mono text and flat sharp blocks. It keeps the book version's own marks so it isn't a copy of Hermes: Roman numerals for the sections and projects, a Greek-key band, inscription capitals (Cinzel) for the motto and the name. `main` still has the open-book site (Codex), the blog and the 3D scenes; this branch removes them.

## The page

One page, in English at `/` and Portuguese at `/pt/` (`src/pages/index.astro`, `src/pages/pt/index.astro`, both rendering `src/views/Site.astro`). Everything it says is in `src/data/site.ts` (both languages, `t(lang)`); the projects are in `src/data/projects.ts`.

- **Layout:** a fixed bar across the top (brand, numbered sections with the one being read underlined, GitHub, LinkedIn, EN · PT, Get in touch; on a phone a Menu button and a full-screen sheet; the bar's fill and blur sit on `.bar::before`, since a `backdrop-filter` on the bar itself would trap the fixed sheet inside it). Then: a full-screen hero (Julius Caesar, the bronze in Rome, seen close, printed in row by row with slow rays behind, who turns all the way round as the hero scrolls away; a huge upper-case headline whose words rise in); Projects (Ode, Ode City, then the sites made for others: `TYPES` sets the order) as a deck of cards (the screenshot, or code, on one side; the project on the other) that stack under the bar as the page scrolls, each held a little lower than the last (plain on phones), Melencolia faint behind the head; a Greek-key band, a full-screen grainy monochrome Laocoön with "Festina lente" in inscription capitals and its gloss, another band; About beside a sticky statue, holding the bio, where I've worked (a ruled list) and what I work with (a logo beside each, `src/data/logos.ts`: simple-icons where it has the brand, a line icon where it doesn't); Contact beside Giambologna's Mercury (the messenger) in an orbit: the address in a solid accent block with a copy button, then LinkedIn, GitHub and the CV as ruled rows. The footer opens with a closing line (the motto, large) and the address as a button, then the brand, columns for the sections, elsewhere and language, the picture credits (`SHOWN` in Site.astro), a back-to-top line, and the name as a Roman inscription (EDVARDVS · DE · BEM in Cinzel) cut out of the marble, sunk into the page's foot. A skip link comes first.
- **Behaviour** (`src/scripts/site.ts`): the section being read underlines its link; the bar fills once the hero is behind (`html.past-hero`); `.reveal` elements fade up once in view (`html.reveals`); the hero's words rise in only under `html.js`, set inline in the head, so without the script the headline simply shows; the menu (the page behind it `inert` while open, focus moved in and back to the button); buttons on hover, their label re-printed letter by letter through random glyphs while a halftone screen in the hover colour swells in steps until solid (`.button::before`, an animated `@property --dot`); copying the email address. Scroll-driven CSS sinks Caesar as the hero leaves and drifts the painting.
- **Pictures:** `src/components/Art.astro` paints a one-ink PNG (`public/art/<id>.png`) through a CSS mask in `--art-ink`, at its own proportions (sizes and credits in `src/data/art.ts`). Project windows are scanned in on arrival. Project screenshots are toned green under a halftone screen (`luminosity` over the accent), and show their own colours under the pointer.
- **Caesar turning:** `npm run turn` (`scripts/turn.mjs`) loads a 3D photo scan of the bronze Caesar on Via dei Fori Imperiali ("Statua di Giulio Cesare" by massimo.riccioni on Sketchfab, CC BY; its download is a .rar holding an OBJ, extracted into `art-src/caesar-rome/source/x/`, kept out of the repo) in headless Chrome with three.js (a dev dependency only). It drops the scan's slivers, the sky caught above his head (everything over `CROWN`, the scan having missed the top of his head, which is rebuilt as a slightly rough dome, `DOME`, in the head's own tone) and a stray line element, frames him close (head to below the knees, `SHOW`, no pedestal; the dots thin out over the foot of each frame, `FADE`, so he dissolves rather than stopping at an edge), renders 48 angles, dithers them all with one tone and lays them out 8 × 6 on one sheet, `public/art/caesar-turn.png`. The sheet is 36 frames, one pixel per dither dot (2040 × 2520, small to decode); the page shows `caesar-still.png` (frame one at full size) through the mask until it scrolls, then `site.ts` draws each frame into a canvas, poured in `--art-ink` and scaled up pixelated (no turn with reduced motion).
- **Monochrome pictures:** besides the dithered ones, `npm run art` makes `MONO` pictures in the Hermes manner (greys from the night to the ink, darkened at the edges, heavy film grain baked in, saved as JPEG): the Laocoön across the screen in the interlude, and a strip of its marble (`laocoon-strip.jpg`) that the footer's giant name is cut out of (`background-clip: text`).
- **Making pictures:** `npm run art` (`scripts/art.mjs`) reads sources from `art-src/` (gitignored), crops, tones and dithers them (ordered Bayer dither in 2px pixels for statues and engravings, round-dot halftone for paintings) into transparent PNGs. Add a picture: put the source in `art-src/`, add it to `ART` in the script and in `src/data/art.ts` with its size and credit. Only use public domain or freely licensed images, and credit them (the footer lists `ART`).
- **Style:** `src/styles/site.css`. Oswald for headlines and upper-case labels, Geist Mono for text (Google Fonts, in `BaseHead.astro`). No frame round the screen (it was Hermes's mark; `--frame` is 0). Accent `--accent` #09db3a (the darks, lines and the pictures' ink `--art-ink` #7cf097 are tinted to its hue); a 4px accent frame round the screen; a faint grain over everything.

## Kept

- `src/pages/api/now-playing.json.ts` (Jellyfin proxy, off on the page). Secrets: `JELLYFIN_URL`, `JELLYFIN_API_KEY`, `JELLYFIN_USER_ID` in `.dev.vars` or `npx wrangler secret put`. Never commit them.
- `src/seo.ts` (JSON-LD Person and WebSite, keep it in step with the CV), `BaseHead.astro` (meta, hreflang, icons), `public/eduardo-de-bem-cv.pdf` (`npm run cv`), project screenshots (`npm run previews`, WebP).
- `public/og.jpg` is the hero at 1200 × 630, screenshot from the built page: re-take it when the hero changes.

## Commands

`npm run dev`, `npm run build`, `npm run preview` (Wrangler), `npm run deploy`, `npm run art`, `npm run turn`, `npm run previews`, `npm run cv`. Deploys to Cloudflare Workers; `main` is protected, so work goes through a branch and a PR.
